FAQ

Straight answers.

Will you run our ads on your own ad accounts?

No. We work inside your own verified accounts, so everything we build stays yours. If anyone offers to run you on their accounts to get past review, that is how whole businesses lose their ads overnight.

What share of revenue do you take?

It depends on your margin and your price. We agree it on the call, in writing, before anything launches. There is no retainer and no setup fee.

What counts as revenue you brought in?

New customers whose first click came from our campaigns, tracked with UTMs, discount codes and your checkout or CRM data. Repeat customers don't count. Both sides see the same dashboard.

What is the minimum ad spend?

£3,000 a month, paid by you directly to the platform. Below that, a share of revenue doesn't cover the work properly.

Can you guarantee our ads get approved?

No one honestly can. Most rejections come from things that can be fixed: the account set up, the wording of the ads and the claims on the landing page. If your product can't legally be advertised, we'll tell you on the call.

Do you work with peptide and research compound brands?

Only where the product is lawful to sell and advertise in the UK and the claims are cosmetic or authorised. We don't take research use only compounds that are really sold for human use.

What happens after the 30 days?

We review the numbers together. Keep going on the same share, move to a Growth Partner retainer, or stop. There is no lock in.

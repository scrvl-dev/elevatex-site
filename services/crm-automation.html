GoHighLevel Setup

Sales Pipeline Architecture

Custom pipelines per offer or business unit. Automated stage progression based on triggers. Never lose a deal to admin again.

Email + SMS Sequences

Welcome sequences, nurture sequences, win back sequences, abandon cart, post purchase. Written in your voice, A/B tested.

FAQ

Questions regulated firms
ask first

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

That is your choice, and we recommend yes. The receptionist can introduce itself as your AI assistant in its opening line. Most callers care far more about getting a clear answer and a booking straight away than about who picks up.

What happens when it can't answer a question?

It does not guess. It only works from the information you approve. Anything outside that is captured as a message with the caller's name, number and question and sent straight to your team, or the call is transferred live if you prefer.

Can it hand over to a human?

Yes. Calls can transfer to a named person or number during the hours you choose, when the caller asks, or when specific topics come up. Outside those hours it takes a detailed message and books a callback.

Where is call data stored, and how does it comply with UK GDPR and call recording consent?

Recordings and transcripts are stored in your own CRM and in the voice platform account set up for your business, so you control access and retention. Every call opens with a recording notice, retention is set to match your policy, and a data processing agreement is in place before anything goes live. For regulated firms we work through your compliance checklist with you during setup.

How long does setup take?

Most receptionists are live within two to three weeks. Week one maps your calls and trains it on your business. Week two is testing against real scenarios. Then it goes live, with close monitoring through the first month.

ElevateX/Industries/UK Social Housing

Marketing for UK social housing

Tender ready brand, capability documents, resident communications and CPD webinar funnels. For providers, maintenance contractors and the businesses around them. Built on real sector work, not a persona deck.

Start a conversation

Social housing is a trust market. Housing associations and local authorities don't buy from adverts; they commission from organisations that look, read and behave like safe hands. Yet much of the sector's supply chain, maintenance contractors especially, presents fifteen years of solid delivery through a dated logo, a brochure that's really a price list, and a website nobody would cite in a tender.

That gap is expensive in both directions. Contractors lose tenders to better presented rivals with thinner track records. Providers struggle to communicate programmes to residents and stakeholders in a way that builds confidence rather than paperwork.

ElevateX has done this work, recently and specifically: a full rebrand, capability brochure and rebuilt website for a social housing maintenance firm, and a CPD webinar funnel, nurture programme and document review automation for a mortgage to rent business. We work as an embedded growth partner, one senior operator across brand, materials, web and communications.

The Problems

Sound familiar?

PROBLEM 01

The brand undersells the delivery

Years of reliable work for associations and authorities, presented like a trade flyer. Commissioners judge capability partly by how capably you present it.

PROBLEM 02

Tender documents built from scratch each time

Every bid reinvents the capability statement, the case studies, the social value story. A proper capability document and evidence library changes the economics of bidding.

PROBLEM 03

Resident communications as an afterthought

Programmes succeed or stall on resident trust, and the letters, guides and updates residents receive are often the least designed materials the organisation produces.

PROBLEM 04

Professional audiences, no education channel

Brokers, advisers and referral partners need to understand your scheme before they'll recommend it, and nobody owns teaching them.

How We Work

A sector that runs on
evidence, not adjectives

The standards we hold

  • Claims are evidenced: case studies, accreditations and social value statements are documented, not decorated.
  • Resident facing materials are written plainly and accessibly. Clarity is a safeguarding issue, not a style choice.
  • We understand the commissioning context: frameworks, DPS, social value weighting, and build materials to match how you're actually scored.
  • Confidentiality as standard across live bids and programmes.
